What matters most today?

Moves the recommendation, not the facts. The fastest option is not always ranked first.

Responsible design in this tool

Environment: every option shows extra miles and estimated CO₂.
Fairness / stakeholders: each option lists who bears the cost: residents (local-street miles, schools nearby), the driver (hours, idling), the partner warehouse, another customer.
Transparency: scores, formulas and assumptions are one click away.
Human oversight: nothing is sent or changed until the dispatcher approves; any option can be chosen over the recommendation.
Responsible data use: drivers are “Driver 01”, no personal data; each item is tagged simulated / official / satellite / needs review.
